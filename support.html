Support

Help with the openrz67 apps.

The apps control the openrz67 trigger, a Bluetooth remote release you build yourself for the Mamiya RZ67. They need the trigger to do anything; see Get started for how to build one.

The app stays on “Scanning for devices”

  • Check that the trigger is switched on. A slow, soft blue pulse means it is on and waiting for a phone.
  • Check that Bluetooth is on and that the app is allowed to use it. On iPhone: Settings → Apps → OpenRZ67 → Bluetooth (on iOS 17: Settings → OpenRZ67).
  • The trigger talks to one phone at a time. Close the app on any other phone that is connected to it.

The connection dropped

The app reconnects on its own when the trigger comes back in range; the status line says “Waiting for trigger” meanwhile. Reconnect makes the app forget the trigger and scan again, which helps if you switched to a different one.

The shutter does not fire

  • A dim, steady blue LED means the trigger is connected. It lights fully while it holds the release.
  • Check the camera cable and that pin 1 is the right way round at both ends.

Bulb mode takes a normal picture

The trigger only holds the release. Set the shutter-speed dial on the camera to B, or it takes one exposure at the dialled speed. The camera closes the shutter by itself after about 60 seconds.
